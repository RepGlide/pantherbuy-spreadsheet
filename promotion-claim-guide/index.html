Promotion Claim Guide

Break down best, verified, updated, coupon, and discount with the context each one needs.

A promotional word is a label, not a fact

Words like best and verified sound like facts. They are labels until they carry a publisher, a date, limits, and a current page.

best

Best by whose measure, and on what date? A “best” with no source and no time is just a preference, not a ranking.

verified

Verified by whom, and when? The word does not do the verifying — a source and a date do.

updated

Updated when? A date matters more than the word. “Updated” without a date could mean anything.

coupon / discount

What limits and conditions apply? A coupon or discount is only meaningful with its terms and its expiry.

?Open item: is any promotional word being read without its publisher, date, or limits?

A worked breakdown

  1. Word: a page says “verified”.
  2. Source: no publisher is shown.
  3. Time: no date is shown.
  4. Result: the word stays a label, not a fact.
