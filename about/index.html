About This Resource

A claim-context and editorial lightbox built around a shared PantherBuy spreadsheet.

What this resource is

This is an independent informational and navigation resource for people who use a PantherBuy spreadsheet. It helps you place promotional words, photos, and price labels back into their source, time, destination, and visible conditions.

What this resource is not

This resource is not the official PantherBuy website, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by PantherBuy or Google. It is not a deal center, product database, store, QC certification, shopping agent, order tracker, or fee calculator.

The claim-context method

Everything here follows four layers: Source (who shows it, where), Time (what date it matches), Destination (where the link now opens), and Visible conditions (the limits and options shown).

How to use it

  1. Open the shared Google Sheet to see the listing references.
  2. Browse the twelve product categories from the home page.
  3. Read the three guides before relying on any listing clue.
  4. Put every claim back into context before relying on it.

Context example — not a promotion, price check, QC review, or verification.
