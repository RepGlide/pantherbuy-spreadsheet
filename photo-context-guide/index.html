Photo Context Guide

Tell listing, destination-page, user-provided, and so-called QC images apart by evidence scope.

Every image has a source

An image means something only inside the source it came from. Read the source before you read the picture.

Listing image

The seller’s presentation on the listing. It shows what the seller wants to show — not the unit you would receive.

Destination-page image

What the page the link opens actually displays. It may differ from the listing image.

User-provided image

A picture someone else shares. Its origin and conditions are not under your control.

So-called QC image

A label, not a guarantee. Without knowing who took it and what it covers, it proves nothing on its own.

?Open item: is any image being read as proof of more than its source shows?

A worked read

  1. Source: a listing image on the sheet.
  2. Scope: the seller’s presentation only.
  3. Not proof: authenticity, material, or the unit you receive.

Context example — not a promotion, price check, QC review, or verification.
