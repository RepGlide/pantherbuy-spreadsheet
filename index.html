Promotion Words Need Expiry and Source

Words That Do Not Stand Alone

best, verified, updated, coupon, and discount mean nothing without a publisher, a date, limits, and the current page.

  • 1

    best. Best by whose measure, on what date? Ask for the source and the time.

  • 2

    verified. Verified by whom, and when? The label alone does not verify.

  • 3

    updated. Updated when? A date matters more than the word.

  • 4

    coupon / discount. What limits and conditions apply? Check the page, not the word.

Photo / Price / Badge

Three Exposures, One Rule

Each exposure shows something — and leaves something unproven.

Photo

Can show
  • What is visible in the frame.
  • The angle the photo was taken from.
Cannot prove
  • Authenticity, material, or condition.
  • What is outside the frame.

Price

Can show
  • A number shown on a page.
  • The currency it sits next to.
Cannot prove
  • The option, quantity, or time it applies to.
  • The final amount after conditions.

Badge

Can show
  • A label displayed on a page.
  • Who shows it.
Cannot prove
  • Certification or quality by itself.
  • That it is current or true.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a PantherBuy spreadsheet?

A PantherBuy spreadsheet is a shared document that compiles listing references, category groupings, and product links formatted for use with agent shopping services. It is a reference to put in context, not an official, live, or complete product catalog.

Is this website the official PantherBuy service?

No. This website is an independent informational and navigation resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by PantherBuy or Google.

Does the claim-context method verify or certify anything?

No. The claim-context method is a reading framework. It is not a deal center, product database, store, QC certification, shopping agent, order tracker, or fee calculator.

What context does a promotional word need?

A word like best, verified, updated, coupon, or discount only means something with its publisher, date, limits, and current destination page. Without those, the word is a label, not a fact.

What can a photo actually show?

A photo shows visible features only. A listing image, a destination-page image, a user-provided image, and a so-called QC image each have different evidence scope, and none of them proves authenticity or quality by itself.

Where do I place an order?

After you have put each claim back into context and rechecked the source page, you continue on an external page to submit the item. This website is a navigation resource and does not process orders itself.
